Who Is Rory?

Meet Rory Your Player 2

Rory is 18 and works with young people with SEND, running One-2-One online gaming sessions and working in person too. He set up Player 2 to help young people who are walking the same path he did.

Join Player 2 Book a One-2-One

“He's a cool character, pretty chilled. I think he represents a lot of young people that hide away.” — Rory, on the mascot

“They're always Player 1 — it's their main focus. I'm just there as the second player, keeping everything safe. That older brother vibe. Someone they can look up to.” Rory, on why it's called Player 2

Rory's Story

Been There Himself

Rory's own journey through education wasn't straightforward. He knows what it's like when school doesn't fit, and that's exactly why Player 2 exists.

“In this industry, there's a lot of people that don't know what it's like, because they've not experienced it. And that's not their fault.”— Rory

  1. Mainstream school

    Where it started.

  2. Specialist provisions

    In and out of a few different ones.

  3. Homeschooled

    For a little while.

  4. No education

    “There was a period of my life where there was no education. I just couldn't.”

  5. One-to-one provision

    Somewhere that worked for him, where he slowly built back up.

  6. College

    Then on to college.

  7. Player 2

    “I decided I want to make a change, and help kids that were like me.”

One-2-One Sessions

What It Feels Like

“It should feel like you're playing with one of your mates. It's chilled.” — Rory

🎮
Led by You

You pick the game. The session revolves around what you're into.

🐢
Your Pace

A bit shy? That's fine. The friendship builds over time, no rush.

💪
No Judgement

Make mistakes and learn from them, without being judged or told off.

🌟
Confidence

An hour to escape, have a good time and not stress about anything else.

📷
Camera Optional

No one has to see your face. Voice chat only is completely fine.

👥
Bring a Mate

Want to play as a group? Bring a friend and squad up together.

One-2-One is also a gentle way in. When you're ready, it's a path to meeting other young people in the main Player 2 server.

For Parents

Keeping It Safe

Being online isn't always safe. Player 2 is set up so parents can have peace of mind that their child is.

✅
DBS Checked

Rory is fully DBS checked and has safeguarding qualifications.

👀
Monitored

Rory monitors the server himself, with moderation software running in the background that flags anything inappropriate.

🚩
Anonymous Reporting

Young people can report anything anonymously. Rory looks back through what happened and steps in when it's needed.

🎂
Ages 10–17

Most members are around 10 to 17, with a real mix of ages, personalities and backgrounds, just like real life.

📷
Camera Optional

No one has to show their face. Young people can join on voice only, at their own pace.
