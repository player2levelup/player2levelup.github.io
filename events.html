How It Works

From Ticket to Prize

Events run on the five core games on the Player 2 server, with Fortnite one of the most popular, and they'll expand to more games over time.

  1. Be a Member

    Events are for Player 2 subscription members. Some tiers come with event perks, and Platinum gets early access.

  2. Grab a Ticket

    Enter with an online ticket. Tournaments like Fortnite Solos and Duos are £10 to enter.

  3. Play

    Compete solo or with a duo partner, in a safe space Rory looks after.

  4. Win

    Real prize funds, paid out in gift cards. A proper esports feel.
