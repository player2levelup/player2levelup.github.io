Who Is Player 2?

A Safe Place to Game Together

Player 2 is an online gaming community for young people, run by Rory. It's built with SEND in mind — somewhere to find people to play with, share your best moments and be part of something, at your own pace, wherever you are in the world.

Rory runs the server and keeps it friendly, respectful and safe, so young gamers can play, chat and hang out without the toxic side of online gaming.

Who's on the server?

Young people aged 13 to 17 who love gaming, with a SEND focus. It's open to everyone: loud squads or quiet one-on-one, however you like to play, there's a place for you here.

Book a Squad Up
Squad Up terms
  1. Squad Up is for Player 2 members. Every player in the squad needs an active Silver, Gold or Platinum membership. Squad Ups are paid for separately and aren't included in any membership.
  2. Every player needs a parent or carer's permission to join, and must be 13 to 17 (Discord's minimum age is 13).
  3. Squad size depends on the game: up to 5 players, plus Rory. Each player joins from their own Discord account.
  4. Cameras are optional. Voice only is completely fine.
  5. Squad Ups are £10 per person for a 1 hour session, booked and paid for in advance through Whop.
  6. Need to cancel or move a session? Give at least 24 hours' notice and we'll rearrange it.
  7. The Player 2 community rules apply to everyone: be respectful and kind, with no bullying and no inappropriate language or content. Rory can pause or end a session if the rules are broken.
  8. Rory is DBS checked and safeguarding trained. Any concern about a young person's safety will be passed on to the right people.
