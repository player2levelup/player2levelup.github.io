Who Is Player 2?

A Safe Place to Game Together

Player 2 is an online gaming community for young people, run by Rory. It's built with SEND in mind — somewhere to find people to play with, share your best moments and be part of something, at your own pace, wherever you are in the world.

Rory runs the server and keeps it friendly, respectful and safe, so young gamers can play, chat and hang out without the toxic side of online gaming.

Who's on the server?

Young people aged 13 to 17 who love gaming, with a SEND focus. It's open to everyone: loud squads or quiet one-on-one, however you like to play, there's a place for you here.

🎮 For Young People

What is Player 2?

A safe, friendly gaming community on Discord, run by Rory. It’s built with SEND in mind, so you can game, chat, share clips and hang out at your own pace, without the toxic side of online gaming.

Who can join?

Anyone aged 13 to 17 who loves gaming. A parent or carer needs to give permission and sign you up. 13 is Discord’s minimum age, so nobody younger can join.

What do I need to join?

A Discord account, and a parent or carer to sign you up through Whop. Once you’re signed up, you get access to the Player 2 Discord server.

Do I have to turn my camera on or talk?

No. Cameras are always optional and nobody will pressure you to turn yours on. Plenty of people just use voice, or stick to typing in the chat channels. Go at whatever pace feels right for you.

What games can I play?

Rory sets up channels for the games the community plays, and Gold and Platinum members can vote in polls. Your game channels are unlocked to match the age rating (PEGI) your parent or carer is happy with.

What is a One-2-One?

50 minutes of gaming with Rory, just you and him. Pick a game, try something new or just chat games. It happens in a private voice room on Discord and it’s led by you. A parent or carer books it.

What is a Squad Up?

Bring your friends and game with Rory for 50 minutes. It’s for 2 to 5 players plus Rory, and everyone in the squad needs to be a Player 2 member.

Are sessions recorded?

One-2-One and Squad Up sessions with Rory are recorded (audio only) to keep everyone safe, and Rory tells you at the start. Recordings are deleted after 30 days. Public voice channels aren’t recorded.

Can I share my phone number or socials?

No. Keep things like your surname, school, address, phone number and other social media private. The server automatically blocks phone numbers and contact details, and if anyone asks you for them, tell a moderator.

What if someone upsets me or something doesn’t feel right?

Tell a moderator, use Discord’s report button, or tell your parent or carer. You won’t be in trouble for speaking up. The server isn’t watched 24 hours a day, so if nobody’s online and you need to talk, Childline is free on 0800 1111. If you’re in danger, call 999.

What happens when I turn 18?

Player 2 is for 13 to 17 year olds, so your membership ends when you turn 18.

🛡️ For Parents & Carers

Is Player 2 safe?

No online space can promise to be completely safe, so Player 2 is built to be as safe as we can make it. It’s a members-only, moderated Discord server run by Rory, who is DBS checked and safeguarding trained. Automatic filters block harmful language and contact details, an activity log helps us check what happened if a concern is raised, and only approved bots are allowed. The full details are in our policies.

What is Whop?

Whop is the secure online platform we use for memberships, session bookings and payments. A parent or carer signs up and pays on Whop, and it’s where you manage or cancel a membership. Whop handles card payments, so we never see or store your full card details. It also links to Discord, so your child gets the right access in the Player 2 server.

How do I sign my child up?

A parent or carer signs up and pays on Whop at whop.com/player2. Choose a membership, answer a few quick questions, and your child gets access to the Player 2 Discord server.

How much does it cost?

Silver is £15, Gold £25 and Platinum £32 a month. Memberships roll monthly and you can cancel any time. One-2-One sessions are £20 for 50 minutes (members get 15% off, or 20% off with Platinum) and Squad Ups are £10 per person.

How does the free trial work?

Register on Whop now and nothing is taken today. On 2nd December everyone is let into the Discord and the 5 free days of Platinum start. Cancel in your Whop account before the 5 days are up and you won’t pay anything. Whop emails you the day before your first payment. Otherwise it carries on at £32 a month.

Can I join the server too?

No. The server is just for young people aged 13 to 17, so parents and carers don’t join. You can contact Rory at any time at player2levelup@gmail.com, and Discord’s Family Center lets you see a summary of your child’s activity.

Who can message my child?

Rory and moderators don’t send private messages to young people unless you’ve agreed it in advance. We recommend setting your child’s Discord privacy settings so only friends can message them. The server also blocks attempts to share phone numbers or move chats off Player 2.

Are sessions recorded, and why?

One-2-One and Squad Up sessions are recorded (audio only) to protect your child and Rory, and you agree to this when you book. Recordings are stored securely, kept for 30 days and then deleted, unless they’re needed because a concern has been raised. Public voice channels aren’t recorded.

How do you decide which games my child can play?

Game channels are unlocked to match the highest PEGI age rating you’re happy with. You can read how this works in our Age-Appropriate Games Policy.

Is Player 2 right for a young person with SEND?

Player 2 is built with SEND in mind. Sessions are led by your child and go at their pace, cameras are optional, and Rory has been through SEND education himself, so he knows the system from the inside. A One-2-One is a gentle first step before joining the community.

How do I cancel a session or our membership?

For a session, email player2levelup@gmail.com at least 48 hours before to move it or get a refund. Sessions cancelled with less than 48 hours’ notice, or missed, are charged in full. You can cancel a membership any time in your Whop account, and it stays active until the end of the month you’ve paid for.

What if I have a concern?

Email Rory at player2levelup@gmail.com. Any safeguarding concern is dealt with under our Safeguarding Policy and passed to the right services when needed. If a child is in immediate danger, call 999.

Where can I read your policies?

All our policies are on the Our Policies page, including safeguarding, online safety, data protection and cancellations.
