Who Is Player 2?

A Safe Place to Game Together

Player 2 is an online gaming community for young people, run by Rory. It's somewhere to find people to play with, share your best moments and be part of something — wherever you are in the world.

Rory runs the server and keeps it friendly, respectful and safe, so young gamers can play, chat and hang out without the toxic side of online gaming.
